Everything you need

A contacts app built for the modern world

✨

Stunning Dark UI

Glassmorphism cards, animated gradient backgrounds, mouse-tracking spotlight, floating particles, 3D perspective tilts — every detail crafted.

📱

Rich Contacts

Multiple phone numbers, emails, addresses, social profiles, company info, birthday, notes, groups, and starred favorites.

🌎

Truly Cross-Platform

Windows .exe, macOS .dmg, Linux .AppImage, deploy anywhere as a web app, or package for Android/iOS with Capacitor.

☁

Cloud Sync (Optional)

Built-in sync engine with Express + SQLite server. Timestamp-based conflict resolution keeps contacts in sync across your devices.

⚡

Local-First & Fast

IndexedDB via Dexie.js stores everything locally. Works fully offline. No account required. Your data stays on your device.

🎨

Delightful Micro-Interactions

Confetti burst on new contacts, star bounce animation, magnetic FAB, scroll parallax, staggered entrances, and smooth toast notifications.
